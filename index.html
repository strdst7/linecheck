            Simulated scenarios. Every customer, address, line, visit, and measurement in Linecheck is fictional and built for demonstration. No provider data, customer record, or network telemetry is used. Time and parts figures are estimates, not quotes.

            Method and limits

            How Linecheck decides

            Four checks are scored against a fixed table of twelve possible faults. A fault only appears in the brief when a check actually supports it. A clean line with a healthy router cannot produce a drop-cable diagnosis, because no answer would justify it.

            Ordering, not proving

            Ranking decides what to look at first. It does not prove a cause. Every item in the brief carries the answer or record that pointed to it, so the technician can disagree with the reasoning line by line.

            Uncertainty is shown, not hidden

            Unanswered checks become "Not yet established". A brief stays Incomplete until speed at the router and line status are both measured, because a wiring conclusion without either is a guess dressed as a finding.

            What is fake here

            The customers, addresses, prior visits, neighbour lines, and measurements are synthetic. The decision table, the gaps, and the handoff logic are real and run in the browser.
